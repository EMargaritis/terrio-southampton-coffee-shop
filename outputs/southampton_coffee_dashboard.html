    High = top quartile of the share of residents aged 25 to 34 across all 152 Southampton LSOAs (17.9% or more). An area is shortlisted when at least 50% of its land lies outside the zone. Each share describes the whole area.

    Borderline: share within 0.5 points of the cut off, or under 60% of land outside the zone.
